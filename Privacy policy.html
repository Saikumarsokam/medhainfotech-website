1. Introduction

Medha Infotech (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the 4X4 Inclinometer mobile application handles information when you use our app.

The 4X4 Inclinometer app is designed to help users view vehicle tilt and inclination angles using compatible sensors available on their Android devices.

2. Information We Collect

The app is designed to operate without requiring users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or home address.

The app may access device motion sensors, such as the accelerometer and gyroscope, to calculate and display inclination or tilt angles.

If processed locally: Sensor readings are used to provide the app's functionality and are not transmitted to our servers.

We do not intentionally collect or store personal information through the app, unless a future feature or third-party service is introduced and disclosed accordingly.

3. Device Permissions and Sensors

The app may use supported device sensors to measure tilt and orientation. Sensor access is used only to provide the app's intended functionality.

The app does not require access to your contacts, SMS messages, or phone call history for its core inclinometer functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

If third-party services, such as advertising or analytics services, are added to the app, their data collection and sharing practices will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

The app is designed to process sensor readings locally on your device. We do not operate a server to collect these readings under the current design.

We take reasonable steps to maintain a privacy-conscious application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Under the current design, sensor readings are used for real-time calculations and are not retained on our servers.

If the app stores any settings or other information locally on your device, you can generally remove that information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to the Android operating system's behavior.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app is designed to operate without collecting personal information, we do not intentionally gather children's personal information through its core functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on Android system components to access device sensors. These system components are governed by the applicable platform terms and privacy practices.

If the app integrates third-party SDKs or services in the future, this policy will be updated as necessary to describe their relevant data practices.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. Any revised version will be published at the privacy policy URL, with an updated effective date.
